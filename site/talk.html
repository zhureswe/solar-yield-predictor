Can a small forecast beat the plan?

Every day the plant must send the grid a plan for tomorrow, hour by hour. The sun does not follow a plan.

  • Every kWh of miss costs money.
  • My question: can an honest forecast make the miss smaller?

Trap 2 · report day

Method

Three simple forecasts

  • Yesterday's real output
  • 7-day median
  • The plant's plan

Two weather models

  • Line: sun + temperature
  • Small random forest
Rule: learn only on the past; test on days the model never saw.
